# Synthetic transaction velocity investigation procedure

Version 1.0. Research demo only. No real customer information.

Repeated payments within a short time window can indicate suspicious transaction velocity. Compare the signal to local account behavior and merchant settlement schedules. Legitimate batch payments and periodic subscriptions can produce similar patterns.

Check the model version, verify evidence freshness, document missing context, and ask an authorized human reviewer to assess the case. Never treat the model score or a graph association as proof of fraud. The platform does not freeze accounts or block transactions.
